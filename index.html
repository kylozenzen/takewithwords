The difference

Every other tool helps creators post more. Take With Words helps people who aren't creators post at all, in their own words.

Built for avoidance, not ambition

Small daily doses instead of content calendars. Prompts come from your actual work, so there's always something true to say.

Your words. Not AI's.

Other tools are racing to write your posts for you. This one won't. Your voice is the point.

A confidence tool, not a content tool

Success is a post you actually sent, not a follower count. The checkup is there to help you finish, not to rank you.

What's in the bottle

Everything a nervous first-time poster needs. Nothing they don't.

Nobody's asking you to post.

You don't have to. If things are good, keep doing what you're doing.

But if you want people to know what you're good at, the work has to leave your head eventually. Take With Words makes that small, regular and a lot less weird.
